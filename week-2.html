Discussion

This was my first experience of using MuseScore Studio for music transcription. Its notation view resembles a printed score, so I found it easy to compare the notes entered in MuseScore Studio with those in the source PDF. Its menus are straightforward to navigate, making it easy to perform basic notation tasks, such as selecting note durations, entering pitches and adding rests. Furthermore, the playback function helped me identify obvious pitch and rhythmic errors. However, details such as dynamics, slurs and accidentals required more time to adjust. I found it particularly easy to make mistakes when adding slurs in passages with more complex notation.

Overall, I found MuseScore Studio useful for basic music transcription and for connecting notation with sound. However, reproducing the finer details of a score requires patience and a deeper understanding of both musical notation and the software’s advanced features.
