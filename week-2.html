Discussion and reflection

This was my first experience of using MuseScore Studio for music notation. Its view resembles a printed score, so it is easy to compare the notes entered in MuseScore Studio with those in the PDF. Its menus are convienient to understand, making it easy to finish my basic notation tasks, such as selecting note durations and adding rests. Furthermore, the playback function helped me identify obvious pitch and rhythmic mistakes. However, details such as dynamics, slurs required more time to adjust. Making mistakes is usual when adding slurs in more complex notation.

Moreover, I recognise that a transcription may sound like the original piece, but this does not mean it has fully details. This is because even if the pitch and rhythm are correct, incorrect backgroud sound may still affect the people’s perception of the music. Therefore, listening to the music can be a method of verification, but should not be the only way to judge. We need to check transcriptions in more the one steps, including listening and comparing each bar.
