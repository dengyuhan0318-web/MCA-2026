Task 2: Comparison of elements in MusicXML and MEI files

1. Note: <note> → <note>

FeatureMusicXMLMEI
AttributesOptional id, default-x, default-y. Pitch and duration are child elements.pname, oct, dur, xml:id, stem.dir occur on this note.
Parentmeasure in this partwise file; part in timewise MusicXML.beam here, inside layer and staff; also permitted directly in layer or chord.
Childrenpitch, duration, type, voice, staff, notations.This first note is empty; permitted children include accid, artic and verse.

Implication: the first B4 semiquaver has MusicXML duration=1 with divisions=4 (one quarter of a quarter note), but MEI dur="16" records its written value. Editing pitch requires child elements in MusicXML and attributes in MEI. A rest is a MusicXML note/rest, but a separate MEI rest.

<note default-x="82.99" default-y="-20">
  <pitch><step>B</step><octave>4</octave></pitch>
  <duration>1</duration><voice>1</voice><type>16th</type>
  <stem>up</stem><staff>1</staff>
  <!-- beam and notations omitted -->
</note>
<note xml:id="l3xgubk" dur.ppq="1" dur="16"
      oct="4" pname="b" stem.dir="up" />

References: MusicXML note; MEI 4 note.

2. Measure: <measure> → <measure>

FeatureMusicXMLMEI
AttributesRequired number; optional implicit, width and id.Optional n, xml:id, metcon, left and right.
Parentpart in this partwise score.section in this score; ending is another permitted parent.
Childrennote, attributes, direction, backup, barline.staff, mNum and control events such as slur; notes sit deeper inside staff/layer.

Implication: MEI separates simultaneous streams into staff and layer containers; MusicXML uses voice/staff values and timing movements such as backup. The pickup’s implicit="yes" hides its MusicXML number, while MEI metcon="false" records incomplete meter. These attributes express different facts.

<part id="P1">
  <measure number="0" implicit="yes" width="187.36">
    <!-- attributes, notes, direction and backup -->
  </measure>
</part>
<section xml:id="u1p8nga7">
  <measure xml:id="s1nf4hxw" metcon="false" n="0">
    <mNum xml:id="b1t0sw0" />
    <staff n="1"><layer n="1"><!-- events --></layer></staff>
    <staff n="2"><layer n="5"><!-- events --></layer></staff>
    <!-- dynamics, tempo, slur and fingering omitted -->
  </measure>
</section>

References: MusicXML measure (partwise); MEI 4 measure.

3. Slur: <slur> → <slur>

FeatureMusicXMLMEI
AttributesRequired type (start/stop/continue); optional number, placement, bezier-x and bezier-y.startid and endid here; timestamp endpoints are also possible. Optional curvedir, bezier and bulge describe the curve.
Parentnotations, inside each endpoint’s note.measure here; a single element points to both endpoint notes.
ChildrenAlways empty.Empty in this export; curve children are permitted.

Implication: MusicXML start/stop markers must be paired, using number to distinguish overlapping slurs; MEI references must resolve to note IDs. Renaming an endpoint ID requires updating the MEI slur. Both files preserve 15 slurs, although this MEI export omits the first slur’s MusicXML Bézier coordinates, allowing engraving software to calculate its shape.

<notations>
  <slur type="start" bezier-x="23.945686"
        bezier-y="13.764924" number="1" />
</notations>
<!-- on the endpoint note: -->
<notations><slur type="stop" number="1" /></notations>
<slur xml:id="m8v21cg" startid="#l3xgubk" endid="#j18ftah4" />

References: MusicXML slur; MEI 4 slur.
