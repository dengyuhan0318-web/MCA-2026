Reflection

This week, I have added genre and licence information to the MEI file and presented the metadata alongside the rendered score. To make it easier for readers to understand and locate this information, we could display it on a web page. This makes it more convenient to cite sources and licences. If this information is only included in the MEI file, the general reader may not open it to view it. However, if the presentation is too brief, important details may be omitted. Therefore, a concise introduction, detailed explanations and access to the original metadata are all essential.
