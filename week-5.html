Metadata schema and MEI header

Task 1 : Create a Metadata Schema

I selected the following eight metadata elements to describe the music, its source and the digital encoding:

  • Title: The name of the encoded excerpt — Rondo alla Turca, bars 1–15.
  • Artist (composer): The person who composed the music — Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart.
  • Publisher: The publisher of the source edition used for my transcription — Drew Weymouth.
  • Encoder: The person responsible for the digital encoding — Hana Deng.
  • Encoding date: The date recorded for the digital encoding — 6 October 2026.
  • Work identifier: The Köchel catalogue number of the parent work — K.331/300i.
  • Work title: The parent work containing the excerpt — Piano Sonata No. 11 in A major.
  • Performance medium: The instrument used to perform the music — piano.
